Divine Influence And Auspicious Being

The sacred roots of our name, destiny, well-being and cosmic order.

DAIVA

Represents divine influence, fate or the "unseen hand" of destiny. It is often associated with the three supreme forces Brahma, Vishnu and Eshwar, and the cosmic order they maintain.

SWASTI

Derived from the Sanskrit su (good) and asti (being), it translates to "well-being" or "auspiciousness". It represents the core meaning of the Swastika symbol, the movement of the sun and alignment of cosmic energy.

AWAKENING TURN

AWAKENING TURN – KSACT

Koti SubSri AnnapurnaDevi Charitable Trust – Committed to identifying academically talented, sincere and deserving school students from financially disadvantaged families, and supporting them in their educational journey.

  • We believe that financial circumstances should never determine how far a child's talent can take them.
  • Through thoughtful identification, responsible educational support and continued encouragement, we strive to help promising young minds remain in school, pursue their aspirations and build a better future for themselves, their families and society.
  • KSACT was established by our family in August 2021, following the passing of our two beloved and elderly ladies Late Sri Annapurna Devi and Late Sri MahaLakshmi Devi.
  • What began from a deeply personal moment of loss was transformed into a lasting commitment to serve society.
  • In memory of our two great ladies and inspired by the belief that education can transform lives, The Trust focuses on discovering talent where financial circumstances may hide it.
  • There are many children with intelligence, dedication, curiosity and the determination to succeed, yet their educational journey can be challenged by circumstances beyond their control.
  • Their potential deserves to be recognized, not overlooked.
  • Studying six students through our KSACT as of now.
  • KSACT motto is Recognizing Talent – Supporting Education – Transforming Lives

Hindu Tradition

SPIRITUAL EVENTS – HINDU TRADITION

Sacred practices for purification, prosperity, protection, and spiritual connection.

Worship

Daily Worship – Puja's

  • Shudh of puja place post self-purification (bath)
  • Sindhur and/or tilak, and offering water, flowers and incense
  • Lighting diya (lamp) and sandhya vandan
  • Praying mother, grand-mother, father, grand-father and family deity
  • Chanting gayatri mantra, lingastakam, hanuman chalisa, subrahmanya astakam, ganapati stotram, ayyappa stotram, narasimha stotram, vishnu sahasranamam, lalitha sahasra namam, sani stotram, venkateswara stotram, etc.
  • Aarti (light offering ritual)

Fire Rituals

Homa & Yajna – Fire Rituals

Vedic fire ceremonies are conducted to purify the environment and uplift spiritual energy. Through the sacred fire, prayers and offerings are conveyed to the divine for prosperity, protection, and inner transformation.

Special

Satyanarayan Puja / Special Observances

Performed to express gratitude or seek blessings for auspicious occasions — home warming, new ventures, birthdays, or marriage anniversaries — these rituals bring divine harmony to family life.

Festivals

Festivals & Celebrations

We celebrate major Hindu festivals including Pongal, Regional New Year, RangBhari Holi, Maha ShivaRatri, Sri RamaNavami, Ganesh Chaturti, Dushera Nava Ratri, Diwali, Krishna Janmashtami, Etc. with traditional rituals, devotional songs and community engagement that foster joy and unity.

Practice

Meditation & Chanting Sessions

Guided meditation, chanting of mantras such as Gayatri Mantra, Hanuman Chalisa, and Mahamrityunjaya Mantra cultivate stillness, positive energy, and mental clarity.

Study

Spiritual Discourses & Study Groups

Regular discussions on sacred texts like Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads and Ramayana guide seekers toward self-realization, ethics, and a deeper understanding of dharma (righteous living).

Seva

Community Seva (Service)

True spirituality lies in serving others, our seva programs include food donation (annadanam), charity drives and environmental care embodying the ideal of "Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu"

May all beings be blissful with good health, wisdom, caring, sharing, mind peace and happy.
