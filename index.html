About

Three meanings behind our name

Daiva Swasti is a sacred name of divine well-being and cosmic blessing — rooted in destiny, auspiciousness, and devotion.

DAIVA

Represents divine influence, fate, or the "unseen hand" of destiny. It is often associated with the three supreme forces—Brahma, Vishnu, and Mahesh—and the cosmic order they maintain.

SWASTI

Derived from the Sanskrit su (good) and asti (being), it translates to "well-being" or "auspiciousness". It represents the core meaning of the Swastika symbol: the movement of the sun and the alignment of cosmic energy.

Services

Spiritual offerings in Hindu tradition

From daily pujas and fire rituals to festivals, meditation, and seva — sacred practices for blessing, purity, and community.

Daily Worship – Pujas

Devotional worship offered with sincerity to invite grace, protection, and harmony into daily life.

Homa & Yajna – Fire Rituals

Vedic fire ceremonies purify the environment and uplift spiritual energy, conveying prayers for prosperity, protection, and inner transformation.

Satyanarayan Puja

Special observances for gratitude and blessings on auspicious occasions — home warming, new ventures, birthdays, or marriage anniversaries.

Festivals & Celebrations

Diwali, Nava Ratri, Maha Shiva Ratri, Krishna Janmashtami and Holi — celebrated with rituals, devotion, and community joy.

Discourses & Study

Discussions on the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, and Ramayana guide seekers toward self-realization, ethics, and dharma.

Community Seva

Food donation (annadanam), charity drives, and environmental care — embodying "Lokah Samastah Sukhino Bhavantu".
